Aetherbow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Aetherbow ("the app", "we") is an offline archery marksman game. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not require an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your game progress (such as scores, levels, and settings) locally on your device using the device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Third parties, ads, and analytics

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services, and does not share any data with third parties.

Permissions

On Android, the app declares the standard Internet permission, which is part of the underlying app framework. The game does not use it to send or receive your personal data.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, the app is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
